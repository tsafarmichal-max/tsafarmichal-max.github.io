Kinder

One kindergarten. Three perspectives.
One coordinated system.

Kinder connects kindergarten managers, staff, and parents in one shared system for attendance, daily changes, staff updates, and parent communication. Each role sees what changed and what needs action.

Research

What I found inside one kindergarten.

I visited a municipal kindergarten and interviewed the lead teacher and an assistant teacher to understand how information moved through the day.

1 kindergarten · 2 staff interviews · On-site observation

01

Work updates mixed with greetings and stickers.

Dedicated WhatsApp groups existed, but operational information still shared the same chat with social messages — no clear categories or priority.

→ Led to Staff Updates

02

Key daily information still lived on paper.

Attendance, schedules, pickup forms, notes, and reference information were spread across sheets, boards, and binders.

→ Led to Attendance & Daily Exceptions

03

Most coordination ran through the lead teacher.

Staff, parents, safety, supplies, events, schedules, and reporting all required her attention.

→ Led to Manager Overview & Time Savers

Automations

Automation without the technical setup.

Kinder hides triggers, conditions, and actions behind ready-made Time Savers written in plain language. Managers adjust the timing, audience, and approval settings without building a workflow from scratch.

Complex logic stays in the background. The user stays in control.
